Frost settings

Blurring and strength are in the toolbar popup. These are the rest.

Open chat

  • A chat you open stays blurred until you click it or start typing. Opening another chat blurs it again.

  • No mouse or keyboard use inside the chat.

  • Typing shows it again straight away.

Blur and reveal

  • Point at one chat, message or the typing box to see it.

  • Blurred photos lose their colour too, so faces are harder to make out.

  • Stops a mouse passing over the chat list from flashing every chat.

Hide the whole window

Blurs everything in WhatsApp Web until you click it. You can also do this any time from the popup or with the shortcut below.

  • You switch tab or app, or minimise Chrome. Also keeps tab previews and taskbar thumbnails blurred.

  • No mouse or keyboard use in the WhatsApp tab.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Turn blurring on or off
  • Hide or show the whole window

What Frost touches

  • Only web.whatsapp.com, and only its look: blurring is plain CSS, switched on by attributes on the page’s root element and on the open chat panel.
  • Frost’s scripts never read your chats, names or photos, and never run inside WhatsApp’s own code. They only notice where you click or type (in the open chat, or on a chat in the list) and count the chat rows, so the popup can warn you if WhatsApp changes its layout.
  • No network access: the extension’s pages block every connection, and there is nothing to send to. Settings stay in this browser and are not synced.
  • Blur is visual. It stops people reading over your shoulder; it does not encrypt anything.
